CarePal

CarePal: Family Tails helps dog and cat owners keep everyday care, vaccines, weight records, vet visits, and clinic or grooming card information together by pet. Use the camera or photo library to read cards, labels, certificates, and appointment messages. CarePal creates editable suggestions for care records, vaccines, prevention, refills, and follow-ups. You review and correct everything before saving reminders or calendar events. Weight records can include date, source, and notes. Clinic, grooming, insurance, and license card details can be organized by pet. CarePal is owner-entered and user-reviewed. It provides no veterinary advice and does not diagnose, treat, decide dosage, or replace a veterinarian. Always follow your veterinarian's instructions for dosage and treatment decisions. Features: - Create pet profiles with species, breed, weight, and multi-pet context - Review OCR candidates from cards, labels, certificates, and appointment messages before saving - Track medicines, prevention, vaccines, refills, and follow-ups - Save weight records and clinic or grooming card details - Save owner-reviewed schedules to notifications and calendar events - Standard settings, legal, and support pages No accounts, advertising SDKs, or tracking.
