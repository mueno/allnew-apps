Cookie / External Transmission Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This is a courtesy English translation provided for convenience. The Japanese version is the official text, and in the event of any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails. This page explains cookies, Sign in with Apple, external transmissions, and service-specific information handling on AllNew App Factory Feedback. For the AllNew LLC Privacy Policy, please see the corporate website version.

1. Basic Policy

  • Cookies and browser storage on this service are managed in two categories: "essential" and "functional." Essential cookies are those needed to keep you signed in, prevent abuse, store consent records, receive submissions, handle reports and takedown requests, and respond to disclosure/deletion requests.
  • Functional cookies and browser storage (your Good votes, submission history display, and Poina's returning-visitor greeting) are used only if you choose "Accept all" on the consent banner shown on first visit. If you choose "Essential only," they are not stored, and any previously stored functional data is deleted.
  • You can change your choice (including withdrawing consent) at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page. Withdrawal takes the same number of steps as giving consent.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral targeting, access analytics, third-party advertising SDKs, or cross-site tracking SDKs.
  • If we introduce analytics, advertising, external SDKs, or additional contractors in the future, we will update this page in advance and provide consent or opt-out mechanisms as needed.

4. Provision to Third Parties Located in Foreign Countries

When you use Sign in with Apple, authentication-related information may be sent to Apple Inc. (United States). In addition, chat content may be sent to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) for the POIPOI receptionist's AI responses and first-pass review reports. In accordance with Article 28 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (Japan), before registration and before submission we present: your consent to providing personal data to third parties in foreign countries, the destination country names, information about the personal-information protection systems of those countries, the protective measures taken by the recipients, and how to obtain further information upon request.

If the destination country or protective measures cannot be identified at the time consent is obtained, we will state that fact, the reasons, and reference information helpful to you. If contractors located in foreign countries other than the United States, offshore re-subcontracting, access from abroad, foreign regions, or foreign support arise, we will add the destination names, countries, data items, purposes, and protective measures before production launch.

If we actively offer this service to residents of the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom, China, the State of California (USA), or other regions outside Japan, we will not begin offering it to such regions until the required notices, consents, rights procedures, cross-border transfer clauses, representative designations, and access or regional restrictions under GDPR, UK GDPR, China's PIPL, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable foreign laws are implemented.

5. Service-Specific Submission Information

Please do not include in submissions any names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, health information, special care-required personal information, third-party confidential information, trade secrets, unpublished inventions, ideas planned for filing, or information that infringes third-party rights. Submissions you wish to make public may be published as moderator-reviewed public summaries, statuses, progress reports, and outcome reports. AI is used only as an aid and does not finalize publication decisions; final decisions are made by human moderators.

6. Exercising Rights and Inquiries

Disclosure/deletion requests, account deletion, Apple ID unlinking, suspension of third-party provision, reports, and takedown requests are accepted through the channels on the AllNew corporate website or our prescribed contact points. After identity verification, we respond in accordance with applicable laws and our prescribed procedures.
